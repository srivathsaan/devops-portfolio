About Me

Hello! I'm Srivathsaan, an MSc Financial Technology (Intelligent Process Automation) student at Nanyang Technological University, Singapore. I'm focused on applying machine learning, statistical modelling, and data analysis to problems in financial technology, backed by 3 years of hands-on experience working with production data as a CloudOps Support Analyst at Bounteous.

In that role, I used Python, SQL, and tools like Splunk, Sumo Logic, and CloudWatch to analyze infrastructure and security telemetry across 20+ client environments — correlating logs and metrics to identify abnormal patterns, investigating 200+ incidents, and turning behavioural traffic data into targeted Cloudflare WAF rules. I also built Python automation and dashboards to process operational data and surface insights faster.

On the ML side, I built a deep learning-based Android ransomware detector combining hybrid static/dynamic analysis with ANOVA feature selection, reaching 98.3% binary classification accuracy. I hold a B.Tech in Computer and Communication Engineering (Silver Medal, CGPA 8.87/10.0) and I'm a certified Google Cybersecurity Professional and Cloudflare Accredited Configuration Engineer. I'm now looking to bring this blend of data analysis, machine learning, and real-world systems experience to a Data Science or ML role in fintech.
